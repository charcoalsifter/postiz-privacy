Section 1

About This Application

Postiz Social Manager is a self-hosted social media management application. It allows authorized users and page administrators to connect their Facebook Pages and manage social media content — including creating, scheduling, and publishing posts — through authorized Meta/Facebook APIs.

This Privacy Policy describes how Postiz Social Manager ("we," "us," or "our") processes personal information when you use Postiz Social Manager to connect and manage supported social media accounts.

This application does not replace or modify the Meta/Facebook platform in any way. It acts as an authorized integration that uses Meta's officially published APIs and authorization mechanisms.

⚠️ Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to the Postiz Social Manager application and its authorized use of the Meta/Facebook Platform. It does not apply to the Meta/Facebook platform itself. Please review Meta's Privacy Policy for information about how Meta processes your information.

Section 2

Facebook Login & Meta API Access

Postiz Social Manager uses Facebook Login (provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.) to allow you to authorize the application to access specific Facebook features on your behalf.

How Facebook Login Works

  • You initiate the connection — the application redirects you to Meta's authorization dialog.
  • Meta authenticates you — you log in directly through Meta's own interface; we never receive or store your Facebook password.
  • You choose what to authorize — Meta presents the specific permissions that the application is requesting. You can review and accept or decline.
  • We receive an access token — if you approve, Meta provides us with an authorization token representing your granted permissions.
  • You can revoke access at any time — through your Facebook App Settings, you can remove the application's authorization.
🔒 We do not receive your Facebook password at any stage. Authentication is handled entirely through Meta's secure authorization system.

What We Can Access

The application only accesses Facebook data within the scope of permissions you explicitly grant through the Meta authorization flow. The application is designed for Facebook Page management on behalf of users who administer those Pages. It does not access your personal Facebook timeline, personal photos, friends list, or other personal data unrelated to the authorized Pages.

Section 4

Facebook Permissions We Request

Postiz Social Manager requests only the Facebook permissions required to provide its Facebook Page management functionality. The permissions that may be requested include:

📋 pages_show_list ✏️ pages_manage_posts 💬 pages_manage_engagement 👁️ pages_read_engagement 🏢 business_management

Why Each Permission Is Needed

  • pages_show_list — to display the list of Facebook Pages you administer and allow you to select which Pages to connect.
  • pages_manage_posts — to publish, schedule, and manage posts on your connected Facebook Pages.
  • pages_manage_engagement — to manage engagement (such as responding to comments) on authorized Pages, where this feature is used.
  • pages_read_engagement — to read engagement metrics on authorized Pages, where this feature is used.
  • business_management — to access Pages associated with Business Manager accounts where required by the Meta API integration.
✅ We do not request permissions unrelated to Page management, including permissions for your personal profile, friends list, birthday, gender, personal photos, or other personal data.

Section 6

Access Tokens & Security

To maintain the connection between your authorized Facebook Pages and the application, Postiz Social Manager processes OAuth access tokens issued by Meta. These tokens represent your authorization and are required to perform API operations on your behalf.

How Tokens Are Used

  • Access tokens are stored to maintain your connected integrations between sessions.
  • Tokens are used only to make authorized API calls to Meta on your behalf.
  • Tokens are associated with your specific account and authorized Pages.

Security Measures

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect authorization credentials and other sensitive information processed by the application. These measures are designed to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

⚠️ Important Security Notice

No Internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot warrant that unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss will never occur. We encourage you to use strong, unique credentials and to review connected applications in your Facebook settings periodically.

If you believe your account or connected integration has been compromised, you can revoke the application's access immediately through your Facebook App Settings.
